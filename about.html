Our Story

Eyad Jamal Fitness was founded with a simple mission: to help people achieve their fitness goals through personalized coaching, science-backed training programs, and unwavering support. With over a decade of experience in the fitness industry, Eyad Jamal has dedicated his career to transforming bodies and changing lives.

What started as a passion for fitness has grown into a thriving community of thousands of satisfied clients who have achieved remarkable transformations. We believe that everyone has the potential to become their best self with the right guidance, support, and dedication.
